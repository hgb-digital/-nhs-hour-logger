NHS Hour Logger Guide
Hudson Brackeen
Uploaded for the MIT Maker Portfolio

#--Description--#
This logger was create to centralize all NHS organization into one website. This logger, however, never reached deployment due to violations with FERPA, which places strict limitations on the hosting of student information. Although I made multiple efforts to work with our local district IT, we never reached an agreement. This logger serves as a template for an organizational volunteer web app, serving to prove viability of future use with local volunteer organizations (especially since it's light weight). 
#--#

#--Development--#
To develop this web app, I ran a XAMPP development webserver (including Apache and a MySQL database) and coded using Visual Studio Code. I mainly used Claude for research purposes, helping me to continue to learn the languages and brainstorm technical workarounds. Throughout the process, I met with my chapter advisor to discuss the future direction of development in order to fit the needs of the organization.
