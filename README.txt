#--Description--#
This logger was create to centralize all NHS organization into one website. This logger, however, never reached deployment due to violations with FERPA, which places strict limitations on the hosting of student information. Although I made multiple efforts to work with our local district IT, we never reached an agreement. This logger serves as a template for an organizational volunteer web app, serving to prove viability of future use with local volunteer organizations (especially since it's light weight). 
#--#

#--Development--#
To develop this web app, I ran a XAMPP development webserver (including Apache and a MySQL database) and coded using Visual Studio Code. I mainly used Claude for research purposes, helping me to continue to learn the languages and brainstorm technical workarounds. Throughout the process, I met with my chapter advisor to discuss the future direction of development in order to fit the needs of the organization.

The MySQL configuration export for the NHS database is also included in this repository. The majority of the information included in that database was generated by Claude for filler data so I could see how it would look while in use. 
#--#

#--Features--#
Users can login, log hours, signup for service opportunities, and see hour statistics per committee. At my high school we have 4 committees within NHS (everyone is a member of one) that each focus on differing avenues of service. Officers can also create service opportunities (title and description) and add as many roles as needed. Roles can have a title, a time of service, an attire, and a set number of signup slots. When logging hours, users can add either Individual Service Hours (ISPs) or Group Service Hours (GSPs). Individual hours require a supervisor while group service hours require you to have been signed up on the service sheet prior to the date of service. Officers can also remove hours they feel are malicious or incorrectly documented.
